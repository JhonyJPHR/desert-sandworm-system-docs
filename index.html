A beginner-friendly, zero-dependency C++ and HLSL creature and real-time GPU granular sand deformation framework for Unreal Engine 5.8. Overview & Key Features

The Desert Sandworm System delivers a colossal 136-meter desert apex predator paired with a physically grounded, GPU-accelerated sand deformation pipeline. Every component is authored in clean, self-contained C++ and HLSL so you can drop the creature or the deformable sand into any project in minutes.

136m Colossal Sandworm & C++ Pose Blender

Includes SK_DesertSandworm, SK_DesertSandworm_PhysicsAsset, 13 animation assets, and USandwormBlendAnimInstance for deterministic C1 Hermite crossfading, spine wave lag (SpineWaveLagFraction = 0.38), and root Z inertia damping without AnimBlueprint complexity.

1024×1024 GPU Ping-Pong Sand Physics

ASandwormSandDeformer manages a 320 m × 320 m sliding window (~31.25 cm/texel) using RT_SandPing and RT_SandPong. Simulates deep trench carving (R), raised lateral berms and subsurface waves (G), dark burrows (B), and a 30 Hz angle-of-repose (33°) avalanche collapse pass.

Any Object Carves the Sand

Attach USandDeformInteractorComponent to any Player Character, Vehicle, or Prop—or let bAutoDeformDynamicObjects scan moving primitives automatically. Enforces strict ground contact (4.0 cm tolerance) and scales depth and rim width proportionally to object size and impact speed.

Autonomous Free-Roam & Combat AI

USandwormCombatAIComponent drives organic surface slithering, 65–105m underground burrow travel, safe Landscape boundary avoidance (RoamMaxRadiusCm = 45000 cm), vibration & line-of-sight perception, and 4 contextual attacks (Spit, Sweep, BodySlam, Lunge).

Reactive 27-Bone VFX & Roar Shockwave

USandwormSandInteractionComponent tracks 27 spine bones against the terrain, drives dynamic armor sand accumulation (SandCoverage), projects screen-space roar shockwaves (M_PP_RoarSonicWave), and spawns 4 primary stateless Niagara VFX systems plus stationary world-space ground wind.

12 MetaSounds & 3-Block Showcase Director

100% original 48 kHz / 16-bit PCM audio wrapped in 12 MetaSounds with 280m logarithmic attenuation (ATT_Sandworm_Colossal) and 9 synchronized delay sliders, showcased by ASandwormShowcaseDirector across a 3-block continuous trailer.

Quick Start Guide (Zero Code — 2 Minutes)

You do not need any C++ or Blueprint programming experience to use this plugin. Follow the steps below to experience the demo level or drop the Sandworm and deformable sand into your own map.

Step 1: Enable the Plugin & Show Plugin Content

  1. In Unreal Engine 5.8, open Edit → Plugins, search for Desert Sandworm System, and ensure Enabled is checked.
  2. Open the Content Browser (Ctrl + Space), click the Settings gear icon in the top-right corner, and check Show Plugin Content.
  3. In the folder tree on the left, navigate to Plugins → Desert Sandworm System Content (mounted at /DesertSandwormSystem/).

Step 2: Play the Included Showcase Map

  1. Open /DesertSandwormSystem/Maps/L_Sandworm_DesertShowcase.
  2. Press Play (Alt + P) or Simulate (Alt + S).
  3. The level's ASandwormShowcaseDirector will automatically play the 3-block FullOneTakeTrailer (Boss Cinematic → Aerial GTA Transition #1 → 11-Clip Animation Gallery → Aerial GTA Transition #2 → Infinite Autonomous AI Drone Demo) with live FPS/GPU telemetry (bShowPerfStats = true).

Step 3: Drag the Sandworm & Deformable Sand into Your Own Map

  1. Apply the Sand Material to Your Landscape: Select your Landscape actor in the Outliner and assign /DesertSandwormSystem/Materials/MI_DesertSand_Dynamic to its Landscape Material slot.
  2. (Optional) Add the High-Resolution Follower Patch: For dense vertex displacement right around the creature even on low-resolution Landscapes, drag /DesertSandwormSystem/Terrain/SM_SandWormFollowerPatch into your level and verify its material is set to MI_DesertSand_FollowerPatch.
  3. Place the GPU Sand Deformer: Open the Place Actors panel, search for SandwormSandDeformer (ASandwormSandDeformer), and drag it into your level. It automatically loads RT_SandPing, RT_SandPong, MPC_SandDeformation, M_SandPhysics_Stamp, and M_SandPhysics_CollapseStep.
  4. Spawn Your Sandworm:
    • Drag /DesertSandwormSystem/Creature/Meshes/SK_DesertSandworm into your level.
    • In the Details panel, click + Add and attach SandwormSandInteractionComponent (drives 27-bone sand carving, Niagara VFX, and MetaSounds).
    • Click + Add again and attach SandwormCombatAIComponent (drives autonomous slithering, burrowing, and combat).
    • Select SandwormCombatAIComponent and check bEnableAutonomousAI = true.
    • Select your SandwormSandDeformer actor and set TargetSandwormActor to your Sandworm. Press Play!

Step 4: Make Any Object (Player, Vehicle, Physics Prop) Carve the Sand

Two Zero-Code Ways to Deform Sand with Any Actor

1. Automatic Scanner (Built-In): ASandwormSandDeformer has bAutoDeformDynamicObjects = true enabled out of the box. Any movable character, vehicle, or physics prop moving faster than 10 cm/s within 140 meters of the sand window automatically carves proportional tracks whenever its bottom touches within 4 cm of the ground.

2. Component Attachment (USandDeformInteractorComponent): For hero actors (like your Player Character, Vehicle, or key physics boulders), select the Actor, click + Add in the Details panel, and add SandDeformInteractor (USandDeformInteractorComponent). It gives you per-object sliders for OverrideRadiusCm, DepthMultiplier, BermMultiplier, and automatically pulls the 320 m × 320 m sand window to the player when exploring distant dunes!

3.1 M_DesertSand_Dynamic — Deformable Desert Terrain & Follower Patch Shader
254 VS / 498 PS / 3 Samplers
Path: /DesertSandwormSystem/Materials/M_DesertSand_Dynamic • Instances: MI_DesertSand_Dynamic (Landscape), MI_DesertSand_FollowerPatch (SM_SandWormFollowerPatch)

What It Does: Reads the live 1024×1024 GPU Render Target (RT_SandDeformation) to displace ground vertices downward in trenches (R channel) and upward along displaced sand berms and subterranean pressure bulges (G channel) via Custom_SandWPO. In the Pixel Shader (Custom_SandNormalWS, Custom_SandAlbedo, Custom_SandRoughSpec), it computes finite-difference 3D normals for trench walls (with an automatic smallImprintBoost so small footprints stay sharp), erases wind ripples wherever sand is churned (A channel), darkens open burrows (B channel), and renders procedural quartz micro-sparkles.

  • RT_SandDeformation (Texture): Points to RT_SandPing. Supplies the live 4-channel deformation data.
  • DesertHeightmapTex (Texture): Points to T_DesertHeightmap_RG8 so the follower patch conforms to the Landscape elevation.
  • bIsFollowerPatch (Scalar 0.0 or 1.0): 0.0 on the Landscape; 1.0 on SM_SandWormFollowerPatch (activating heightmap snapping and seamless perimeter skirt tucking beneath the Landscape).
  • Global Sliders via MPC_SandDeformation: MaxCarveDepthCm (620.0), MaxBermHeightCm (340.0), DeformNormalStrength (1.35), BurrowDarkenStrength (0.94), SandEdgeFadeFraction (0.12), SandDeformExtentCm (32000.0), and SandRTResolution (1024.0).
3.2 M_SandPhysics_Stamp — GPU Batched Multi-Stamp Brush Solver
153 VS / 808 PS / 1 Sampler
Path: /DesertSandwormSystem/Materials/M_SandPhysics_Stamp • Used By: ASandwormSandDeformer::ExecuteStampBatch()

What It Does: An unlit GPU compute-like brush shader drawn onto the Ping-Pong Render Targets. To minimize draw calls, it evaluates up to 8 stamps simultaneously in a single pass (StampPosRadius0..7 and StampStrength0..7) while also applying incremental texel-quantized window scrolling (UVShift) so moving the deformation window never clears existing tracks.

  • PrevSandRT (Texture): The current source Render Target from the Ping-Pong pair.
  • UVShift (Vector): (DeltaU, DeltaV, TexelUV, 0) — scrolls stored deformation pixels when the window recenters.
  • WindowCenterWS (Vector): (CenterX, CenterY, DeformExtentCm, 0).
  • StampPosRadius0..7 (Vector): (U, V, RadiusUV, Mode) where Mode is 0 (CarveAndBerm), 1 (SubsurfaceBulge), 2 (BurrowHole), or 3 (ImpactCrater).
  • StampStrength0..7 (Vector): (CarveStrength, BermStrength, BurrowStrength, BermWidthScale / BulgeStrength). Setting W = 0.40 tightens the raised sand rim right around small dynamic physics objects.
3.3 M_SandPhysics_CollapseStep — 30 Hz GPU Granular Gravity & Avalanche Solver
148 VS / 612 PS / 1 Sampler
Path: /DesertSandwormSystem/Materials/M_SandPhysics_CollapseStep • Used By: ASandwormSandDeformer::ExecuteCollapsePass()

What It Does: Runs a 3×3 neighborhood cellular automaton at a fixed 30 Hz frequency. Whenever a carved trench wall or crater rim is steeper than the sand's natural Angle of Repose (AngleOfReposeDeg = 33.0°), sand flows down the gradient from the raised berm (G) into the trench (R). Once the slope relaxes below 33°, mass flux shuts off completely (zero FP16 terrace drift), preserving footprints and stabilized craters indefinitely.

  • CurrentSandRT (Texture): Source Render Target for the current collapse tick.
  • PhysicsParams0 (Vector): (TexelUV, StepCm, ClampedDeltaTime, Tan(AngleOfReposeDeg)).
  • PhysicsParams1 (Vector): (MaxCarveDepthCm, MaxBermHeightCm, AvalancheDiffusionRate, BurrowClosureRate).
  • PhysicsParams2 (Vector): (AmbientInfillRate, BulgeSettleRate, SurfaceSmoothStrength, DisturbedMaskDecayRate).
  • KeepOpenUV (Vector): (U, V, RadiusUV, Strength) — keeps an active emergence or submerge funnel open while the worm's body passes through, then releases it so the crater walls avalanche inward.
3.4 M_DesertSandworm — Creature Chitin Armor, Shells & Teeth PBR Shader
168 VS / 214 PS / 3 Samplers
Path: /DesertSandwormSystem/Creature/Materials/M_DesertSandworm • Instances: MI_Sandworm_HeadAndArmor, MI_Sandworm_ArmorShells, MI_Sandworm_TeethAndSpines

What It Does: Master PBR surface shader for the 136m Sandworm skeletal mesh. Blends high-resolution BaseColor, Normal, and Packed ORM (Ambient Occlusion, Roughness, Metallic) maps with a dynamic, world-up-aligned desert sand layer that coats the monster when emerging from underground and gradually sheds off while above ground.

  • BaseColorTex, NormalTex, ORMTex (Textures): Dedicated texture sets for the head/armor, dorsal shells, and teeth/spines.
  • SandCoverage (Scalar 0.0..1.0): Driven automatically at runtime by USandwormSandInteractionComponent based on underground vs. surface time (MinAboveGroundSandCoverage = 0.22, SandShedRatePerSec = 0.20, SandAccumulateRatePerSec = 0.35).
  • SandTintColor (Vector Color): Albedo tint of the clinging desert sand grains.
  • SandRoughness (Scalar): Roughness override applied to sand-dusted carapace regions.
3.5 M_PP_RoarSonicWave — Screen-Space Acoustic Roar Shockwave
148 VS / 91 PS / 1 Sampler
Path: /DesertSandwormSystem/Materials/M_PP_RoarSonicWave • Used By: Level PostProcessVolume + MPC_SandwormFX

What It Does: An ultra-fast Post Process shader (MD_PostProcess) that reads the projected 2D screen coordinates of the Sandworm's roaring jaws from MPC_SandwormFX and distorts the air with expanding concentric pressure rings and radial RGB chromatic aberration while keeping the center of the worm's mouth sharp.

  • RoarDistortionIntensity (Scalar): Master amplitude of the shockwave refraction (0.0 when silent).
  • RoarWavePhase (Scalar): Animates the outward travel of the concentric acoustic rings.
  • RoarScreenCenterUV (Vector): (ScreenU, ScreenV, FacingMask, DistAtten) — origin of the shockwave on screen.
  • RoarFacingMask & RoarDistanceAtten (Scalars): Smoothly attenuate the effect based on camera look angle and distance.
3.6 M_Sandstone_Outcrop — 100% Procedural Nanite Sedimentary Rock Shader

Channel Packing in RT_SandPing & RT_SandPong

  • R (Red — Trench Carve Depth): Downward excavation depth (0.0..1.0 maps to 0..MaxCarveDepthCm, default 620 cm).
  • G (Green — Displaced Berm & Subsurface Bulge Height): Upward sand ridge or underground pressure wave height (0.0..1.0 maps to 0..MaxBermHeightCm, default 340 cm).
  • B (Blue — Deep Burrow Tunnel Mask): Marks open emergence and dive holes (0.0..1.0), darkening the pit interior via BurrowDarkenStrength (0.94).
  • A (Alpha — Persistent Churned Sand Mask): Tracks disturbed zones where wind ripples have been flattened.

USandwormCombatAIComponent provides a complete C++ state machine for both unscripted open-desert exploration (Free-Roam AI) and reactive boss encounters (Combat AI), driving smooth transitions through USandwormBlendAnimInstance.

AutonomousSurveyOrRoar (9) Free-Roam Rears up to survey the desert (AnimIdleCombat) or bellows (AnimRoar) before selecting the next organic destination.

Safe Landscape Patrol Area & Soft Boundary Avoidance

On BeginPlay, the AI automatically measures your Landscape bounds and insets them by LandscapeEdgeMarginCm = 25000.0 cm (250 m) while also clamping exploration within RoamMaxRadiusCm = 45000.0 cm (450 m) around RoamBoundsCenterXY. If the worm nears the safe border, ApplySoftBoundaryAvoidance() smoothly curves its heading back toward the interior so it never leaves your playable terrain.

Ready-to-Use Blueprint & C++ Combat Integration Functions

  • Native Unreal Damage (ApplyDamage): The component automatically binds to the owning Sandworm actor's OnTakeAnyDamage delegate and configures combat query collision on SK_DesertSandworm_PhysicsAsset. Simply call Unreal's standard ApplyDamage node on the Sandworm Actor from any projectile, explosion, or melee trace to reduce CurrentHealth (default MaxHealth = 2500.0) and trigger combat, hit reactions, or death!
  • TriggerTestThreat(FVector Location) (CallInEditor / BlueprintCallable): Simulates a target vibration at Location (or 35m ahead if zero) to immediately test combat aggro.
  • ApplyTestDamage(float Amount = 250.0f, AActor* Instigator = nullptr) (CallInEditor / BlueprintCallable): Applies instant test damage in Editor or PIE.
  • SetAutonomousAIEnabled(bool bEnabled, bool bResetBehavior = true) (BlueprintCallable): Starts or stops autonomous AI at runtime.
  • SetRoamBounds(FVector2D InCenterXY, float InMaxRadiusCm = 45000.0f) (BlueprintCallable): Dynamically updates the safe patrol area.
  • ForceAutonomousBurrowNow() (CallInEditor / BlueprintCallable): Forces an immediate dive → underground travel → eruption cycle.
  • GetHealthNormalized(), GetCurrentState(), GetCurrentAIStateName(), IsUnderground(), GetAITrackFocusPoint() (BlueprintPure): Ready for UMG Boss Health bars and camera tracking.
  • OnStateChanged (BlueprintAssignable): Multicast delegate firing (OldState, NewState) on every state transition.

Showcase Director (ASandwormShowcaseDirector)

ASandwormShowcaseDirector runs the cinematic presentation in L_Sandworm_DesertShowcase, managing camera choreography, strict CPU/GPU tick isolation between the 3 desert zones, pure-Slate title overlays, and aerial transitions.

  • ShowcaseMode (ESandwormShowcaseMode):
    • FullOneTakeTrailer (Default): Plays Block 1 (34s Boss Cinematic) → GTA Transition #1 (2.8s) → Block 2 (~28.7s 11-Clip Animation Gallery) → GTA Transition #2 (2.8s) → Block 3 (Infinite Autonomous Free-Roam AI with Adaptive Drone Camera).
    • BossCinematicOnly: Loops the 5-act 54-second Boss Arena choreography (bResetSandOnLoop = false preserves cumulative craters across loops).
    • FreeRoamAIDemo: Jumps straight into Block 3 for unscripted autonomous AI exploration.
  • bShowPerfStats (Default true): Automatically executes stat fps and stat unit on BeginPlay (checking UGameViewportClient::IsStatEnabled so it never toggles off already-active stats). Uncheck it in the Details panel under Sandworm | Showcase to hide the telemetry overlay with one click.
  • GalleryEntries (TArray<FSandwormGalleryEntry>): 11 customizable gallery slots (IDLE COMBAT, SLITHER, ROAR, ATTACK SWEEP, ATTACK BODY SLAM, ATTACK LUNGE, ATTACK SPIT, HIT REACT, EMERGE V2, SUBMERGE, DEATH) with per-clip orbit angles, distances (10800–12800 cm), heights (2500–3200 cm), and focal lengths (28 mm).
  • 3-Step Aerial GTA Transitions (GTATransitionDuration = 2.80s): Ascends in 3 snappy exponential steps (GTAStep1HeightCm = 2500, GTAStep2HeightCm = 8500, GTAStep3HeightCm = 20000), arcs across the desert (+3500 cm) while seamlessly shifting the Sand Deformer target and window anchor at mid-flight, and descends in 3 steps into the next zone.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

1. I placed the Sandworm and ASandwormSandDeformer in my own map, but the ground doesn't deform. Why?

Verify three things: (1) Your Landscape uses MI_DesertSand_Dynamic (or you placed SM_SandWormFollowerPatch with MI_DesertSand_FollowerPatch); (2) TargetSandwormActor and TargetLandscape are assigned on your ASandwormSandDeformer; and (3) Your Sandworm has USandwormSandInteractionComponent attached (or bTrackBonesAutomatically = true on the deformer).

2. Why isn't my physics cube or player character leaving tracks in the sand?

The system enforces strict physical ground contact (DynamicObjectContactToleranceCm = 4.0 cm) so airborne objects never dent the sand. Make sure the bottom of your object's collision primitive touches within 4 cm of the Landscape surface, its Mobility is Movable, and you attach USandDeformInteractorComponent if you want the 320 m × 320 m window to follow your player far away from the Sandworm.

3. How do I use my own Player Character instead of the automated Showcase Camera in L_Sandworm_DesertShowcase?

Select SandwormShowcaseDirector in the level Outliner and uncheck bAutoPlayOnBeginPlay (or uncheck bDriveCinematicCamera, bAutoPossessShowcaseCameraOnBeginPlay, bHidePlayerPawnDuringShowcase, and bHideGameHUDDuringShowcase under Showcase | Camera).

4. How do I hide the green FPS and Frame/GPU timing stats in the top-right corner?

Select SandwormShowcaseDirector in the Outliner, scroll to Sandworm | Showcase in the Details panel, and uncheck bShowPerfStats.

5. Why doesn't the sand deformation window follow my camera when I fly 100+ meters high up in the air?

In AutoSmart mode, AutoSmartMaxCameraHeightAboveGroundCm = 2500.0 cm (25 m) prevents a high-altitude flying camera from pulling the deformation window away from the Sandworm's arena, keeping the worm's craters 100% preserved when viewed from afar. To force the window to always follow the camera at any altitude, switch WindowFollowMode on ASandwormSandDeformer to FollowActiveCamera.

6. Why don't I see the screen refraction shockwave when the Sandworm roars in my custom map?

Add a PostProcessVolume to your level, check Infinite Extent (Unbound), and add /DesertSandwormSystem/Materials/M_PP_RoarSonicWave to Rendering Features → Post Process Materials → Array.
